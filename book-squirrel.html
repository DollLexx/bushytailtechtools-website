Meet Book Squirrel. 🐿️📖

A home for the stories, ideas, and chapters you want to turn into a book.

Capture ideas as they arrive, connect them to a book, build chapters, and preview your manuscript without juggling a pile of separate documents.

💡 Idea VaultCollect ideas and associate them with one or more books.
✍️ Chapter writingDraft, edit, and organize chapters in a dedicated workspace.
📖 Preview & exportReview your manuscript and print or save it as a PDF.
💾 Local databasesKeep writing in SQLite databases with manual backup and restore.
